---
title: Titling of Aggregates
---
The title of the aggregating Work should reflect that of the Manifestation.
The component titles may additionally be added to the aggregating Work as alternative title types (see [Alternative](/appendices/titles/title_types/#sec-alternative)).
They may also be added to any Items as alternative title types.

Titling of aggregates follows the same principles as that of other types of entities (see [Titles and Title Types](/appendices/titles/)).

The title of the aggregate Manifestation forms the basis, but that title may be a “title proper” or a fully or partially devised/supplied title.
(See [Title Proper](/appendices/titles/title_types/#sec-title_proper) or [Supplied/Devised Titles (i.e. Creating titles for untitled/unidentified entities or production material)](/appendices/titles/title_types/#sec-supplied_devised_titles)).

Aggregates may be formed by an archive or institution itself for in-house or other event purposes.
In these instances the title type should indicate it is a devised/supplied title in both the aggregate Manifestation and aggregating Work, as well as the Item.
