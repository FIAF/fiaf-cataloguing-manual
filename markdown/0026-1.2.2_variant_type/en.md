# 1.2.2 Variant Type[^42]<a name="manual-1.2.2"/>

Identify and describe the kind of change from a Work that gives rise to any instance(s) of a Variant. Selection should be made from a controlled list of values. Examples: Cen-
sored; Dubbed; Subtitled; Edited for TV. See [D.2 Variant Type](#manual-D.2).

[^42]: EN 15907, 4.2.2 Attributes of a Variant, variant type; YCR, 2.1.1 Nature of modification (change in content) of expression, p. 48
