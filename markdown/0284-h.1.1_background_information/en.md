# H.1.1.2 Source of data

Regarding the source of intellectual property data, it is recommended to obtain the relevant information from onscreen text, taken from the first official manifestation in the primary country of origin. Historically this has been a theatrical release in the primary country of production, but as that model changes to include simultaneous online publication of moving image works across multiple territories, this recommendation should accommodate this fluidity: capture the data from the best available manifestation source representing the arguable ‘first fixation’ of the work, and document that source in your work record. Alternatively, use information given by the production companies or rights holders, or from other authoritative secondary sources.
