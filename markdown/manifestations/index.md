


!!! abstract "Definition"
    The embodiment of a moving image Work/Variant.
    Manifestations include all analogue, digital and online media.
    Where applicable, Manifestation information can include a description of what the particular Manifestation should ideally contain, regardless of the Items held in the archive. The associated Items need not all be identical copies or share the same technical format.
    For example: The original release running time of a film is 1:30:00, but the Item held at the archive is missing footage so is shorter.

# Moving Image Manifestations

A Manifestation describes an embodiment of a moving image Work or Variant. For a particular release or broadcast, common characteristics of content and technical format can provide a reference for describing the associated Items.

The relationship between a Manifestation and its Items depends on the materials being described and the cataloguing approach adopted. Items may be copies of a particular release, but the manual also accommodates related production materials grouped under a [Pre-Release Manifestation](attributes_of_a_manifestation/index.md#sec-pre_release) and, according to institutional policy, derivatives associated with the same Manifestation as their sources (see [Boundaries between Manifestations](../boundaries/boundaries_between_manifestations/index.md#sec-changes_in_the_format)). Such associations do not imply that every Item has identical content, extent, function, or technical format.

Manifestation-level characteristics provide a reference where they apply to the associated Items. Item descriptions should record the actual characteristics of each object; characteristics should not be assumed to apply solely because the Item is linked to that Manifestation. These allowances do not make a Manifestation an unrestricted grouping of everything related to a Work: the guidance on Manifestation types and boundaries still governs the association.

It may be whole or incomplete or a fragment.

A Manifestation is not exclusively bound to a single Work/Variant, since it can be an aggregate for reasons connected to the publication, to the commercial release, or for mere convenience.
A Manifestation can therefore be associated or linked to more than one Work/Variant, where an institution’s system allows.

For Aggregate Manifestations see [Aggregates (Compilations, Multi-component productions)](../aggregates/index.md).
