3.1.5.3 Sound

Technical specifications relating to the fixation of sound in a moving image Manifes-

tation/Item (see 2.3.4.3 Sound characteristics of a moving image Manifestation). This element is for high-level description of sound on the item; i.e., noting whether it has sound, is silent, etc.

Indicate the presence or absence of sound in the Manifestation/Item, i.e. “sound,” “silent,” “mute”, “combined” or “mixed” etc. Selection should be made from a controlled list of terms. A suggested list, which is open and not exhaustive, can be found in D.7.4 Manifestation/Item Sound Type.

Record only if this information is not captured at the Manifestation level or if required

at the Item level by the system in use.
