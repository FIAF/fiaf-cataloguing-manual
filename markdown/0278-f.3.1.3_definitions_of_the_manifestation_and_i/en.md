# F.3.1.3 Definitions of the Manifestation and Item Entities<a name="manual-F.3.1.3"/>

A Manifestation is the embodiment of a Work/Variant and includes all the analogue or digital forms that are derived from a Work/Variant and bear common characteristics with respect to shared intellectual content and physical format. It may be whole or incomplete or a fragment.

An Item is the physical product of a Manifestation of a Work or Variant, i.e. the physical copy of a Work or Variant. It may be whole or incomplete or a fragment. In the case of purely digital media, an Item is defined as the availability of the computer file, irrespective of the number of backup copies that may exist[^238].

[^238]: Digital medium definition taken from CEN’s “Film Identification – enhancing interoperability of metadata.
Element sets and structures. FprEN 15907:2010 (E)
